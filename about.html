About Me

Data Science & Machine Learning Engineer

Professional Summary

I am a passionate Data Science and Machine Learning Engineer with strong expertise in analyzing complex datasets, discovering hidden patterns, and understanding both linear and non-linear relationships within data. I specialize in transforming raw data into actionable insights and intelligent systems that support decision-making and predictive analytics.

I work extensively with Python, SQL, and Power BI to clean, analyze, visualize, and automate data workflows. I build interactive dashboards using Power BI and Streamlit, enabling businesses and traders to monitor data dynamically and make real-time decisions efficiently.

I have experience integrating APIs and collecting real-time market data from financial platforms, including stocks, forex, cryptocurrencies, and trading systems. I can develop intelligent applications that execute technical analysis strategies, monitor market conditions, and automate financial data processing.

My expertise includes Machine Learning, Deep Learning, and Predictive Modeling using advanced algorithms and frameworks such as XGBoost, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting Machines (GBM), TensorFlow, and PyTorch. I work with both Classification and Regression models to solve complex analytical problems and generate accurate predictive solutions.

I also develop sequence-based deep learning systems using LSTM, RNN, and GRU architectures for time-series forecasting, financial prediction, behavioral analysis, and intelligent automation systems. In addition, I work with computer vision and image understanding models capable of analyzing and interpreting visual data.

My specialized fields include financial market analytics, algorithmic trading systems, technical analysis, and weather data analytics. I build predictive systems capable of forecasting market trends and detecting probabilistic opportunities based on historical and real-time data.

I also integrate economic news APIs and financial news platforms such as FXFactory into trading systems to avoid high-risk trading periods during negative market news and identify potential opportunities during positive economic events. My systems are designed to combine quantitative analysis, market psychology, and AI-driven decision-making.
